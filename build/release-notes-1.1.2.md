# Localtone 1.1.2

Give your playlists their own icons.

- Choose from 32 matching outline icons, including stars, hearts, arrows, a moon, records and headphones.
- Set an icon when creating a playlist, or change it in Playlist settings using the compact icon button beside the name.
- The floating picker keeps the dialog compact, with unboxed icons, even spacing and no extra title or dropdown arrow.
- Use the mouse or arrow keys to choose an icon. Escape closes the picker first; Reset to default restores the regular or dynamic playlist symbol.
- Icons are saved across restarts and included in collection exports and imports. Cancel discards unsaved choices. Existing playlists keep their default symbols until you choose an icon.

Your settings, libraries, music and playlist rules are preserved. This release retains the playback improvements from 1.1.1.

## Updating

Use Settings → About → Check for updates, or download the complete desktop ZIP for your system. Quit Localtone before replacing app files. Keep your existing Localtone-data folder; do not replace it with an empty one. Settings → About → Prepare update… can transfer your data to a separately extracted app folder.

Mac builds are ad-hoc signed, not Apple notarized; Windows builds are unsigned. Matching decoder sources, notices and SHA-256 checksums accompany the release.
